SIGNATURE METHOD

PACE Framework™

Positioning, Access, Connecting, Execution.

P — Positioning

Define the value you create, the business problems you solve, evidence behind your claims and the mandate you want.

A — Access

Identify relevant organisations, market signals and decision-makers—including opportunities not publicly advertised.

C — Connecting

Build purposeful relationships around relevance and contribution, not generic requests or contact collecting.

E — Execution

Track outreach, conversations, feedback and market response; address weak points and recalibrate.

PACE is a strategic framework, not a guarantee of employment. It does not replace capability, evidence, market fit or hiring decisions.
