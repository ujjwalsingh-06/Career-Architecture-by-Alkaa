About the book

A more deliberate way to approach your next career move.

Many professionals approach career change as a sequence of applications, résumé edits and waiting. From Application to Offer invites readers to examine the strategy behind the search: positioning, professional value, market access, networking, opportunity creation, interviews and execution.

The book introduces practical ways to think about the Hidden Job Market and move from reactive job searching toward a more deliberate approach to creating and converting opportunities.

It is intended for professionals who want to take a more structured, market-aware approach to their next career move.

The philosophy

Experience is the raw material. Strategy connects it to opportunity.

The book reinforces the same OPPARC principles: clarify your value, understand the market, build relevant access and execute with intent.
